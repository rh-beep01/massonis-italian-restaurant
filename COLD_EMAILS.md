## 🎯 Target Value Propositions & Angles

1. **0% Commission Direct Ordering:** DoorDash and UberEats take 25%–30% on every order. A dedicated direct pickup & delivery system retains 100% of order revenue and protects customer margins.
2. **Mobile-First Experience for Nottingham & Perry Hall Locals:** Most diners browse and order on their phones while commuting or at home. The demo site loads in under a second, features an instant cart drawer, and lets customers order their favorite dishes (like the legendary Spaghetti Eggrolls) in 3 taps.
3. **Catering & Party Tray Inquiries:** Prominently showcases half-pan & full-pan party trays with direct inquiry forms, unlocking high-ticket revenue for local events, graduations, and game days.
4. **Authentic Digital Ambiance:** Warm Italian trattoria aesthetic (maroon & gold), real Google Maps directions link on Belair Road, table reservation requests, and verified 5-star customer testimonials.

## 💡 Practical Outreach Tips

1. **Personalization Verification:** Before sending, check if Nicole Massoni or Chuck Michael is currently active at the restaurant. Mentioning a specific recent positive review from Nottingham locals can elevate response rates significantly.
2. **Send Times:** The best window for independent restaurant owners is **Tuesday to Thursday between 2:00 PM and 3:30 PM EST** (after the lunch rush and before evening dinner prep).
3. **Low-Friction Ask:** Notice the CTAs don't ask for a 30-minute Zoom call. They simply ask *"Would it be worth a brief chat?"* or *"Open to seeing how you could use this?"* Lowering friction yields higher reply rates.
